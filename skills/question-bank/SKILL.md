# Question bank

Write in the requested language. Use short sentences for that class. Do not invent personal data. Do not mark the draft approved.

Ground every question in the syllabus hint facts and examples. Do not use vague stems that only repeat the chapter title.

Required shape:

1. One How to use section (उपयोग in Hindi).
2. Exactly the requested number of numbered questions of mixed recall and short application.
3. An answer key covering every question.
4. Cite the syllabus locator exactly.

Vary difficulty lightly across the set, but keep every item solvable from the chapter facts given in the tool result.
