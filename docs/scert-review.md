# SCERT review — procedure and sign-off

This procedure was not carried out by SCERT, and the people who built this prototype are not SCERT experts. The sheet below is a template for a real review. An empty or sample signature is not a sign-off.

## When a review would happen

After the automatic checks pass, and after a person who is not the author has spot-checked about 10 percent of the eval cases using `evals/rubric.md`. The spot-check ids from the local run are listed in `evals/results/local-claude-sonnet-5-5.md`. Those ids have not been reviewed.

## What the reviewer would receive

- The draft, in Hindi or English as requested.
- The class, subject, and topic.
- The citation the draft names.
- The automatic checks, including any that failed.
- A statement that the school-data file, if mentioned, is synthetic.

The reviewer would not receive pupil names. The prototype is not supposed to have any.

## What the reviewer would decide

One of: usable in a pilot, usable after the edits written on the sheet, or not usable. A "usable" decision here still does not approve a draft inside the app. An officer does that separately, on that draft.
