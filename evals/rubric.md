# Scoring rubric

Score each draft from 1 to 5. Automatic checks do not replace these judgments.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Factual correctness | A wrong fact a pupil might memorise | Minor slip, or the draft tells the teacher to check the book | Facts match the cited textbook chapter |
| Syllabus fit | Different topic or stage | Related, but the wrong emphasis for the class | Matches the class and the cited chapter |
| Difficulty | Too hard or too easy to use | Usable with edits | A pupil of that class can try every question |
| Language quality | Broken sentences or the wrong language | Understandable, with grammar or term problems | Clear Hindi or English, with the terms the textbook uses |
| Child appropriateness | Unsafe, shaming, or adult | Flat but harmless | Respectful, concrete, and suitable for the class |

A model may propose these scores. A person still spot-checks about 10 percent of the cases. This prototype's spot-check list is not a SCERT review, and the authors are not SCERT experts.

Pass, for the subjective half, means every criterion is 4 or 5. The automatic half passes only when schema, sections, language, reading length, citation, personal-data, and safety checks all pass. Worksheets and question banks also need a complete answer key. A lesson plan does not.
